HOW TO PLAY

  • Choose a game mode and complete all 10 levels.
  • Click tiles to interact — swap, match, or neutralize depending on the mode.
  • Every wrong move increases the Breach Meter.
  • If the Breach Meter hits 100% — mission failed.
  • Score = Base (10,000) + Speed Bonus + Accuracy Bonus + Move Efficiency.
  • Use the Hint button (×3 per level) to highlight a correct placement.
  • Domain Alignment: Click two tiles to swap them. Align each row to one domain.
  • Threat Matching: Click a threat tile, then its matching control. Wrong pair = breach.
  • Incident Response: Click threat tiles to neutralize them before breach hits 100%.
